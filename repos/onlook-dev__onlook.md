### [v2.0.2](https://github.com/onlook-dev/onlook/releases/tag/v2.0.2) — Onlook 2.0
<sub>2026-11-19 · 2d ago</sub>

> Onlook is a free, open-source editor for designers and visual engineers who shape their product in real code. This release is the Onlook desktop app: the Electron app, the canvas, and the layer that runs Claude Code or ChatGPT on your Mac.
> - **Download:** [onlook.com/install](https://www.onlook.com/install). The app is free and works with the Claude or ChatGPT subscription you already have. macOS on Apple silicon and Intel.
> - **Build from source:** [SOURCE_BUILD.md](https://github.com/onlook-dev/onlook/blob/main/SOURCE_BUILD.md). A source build runs without an Onlook account, cloud keys or a `.env` file.
> - **Docs:** [docs.onlook.com](https://docs.onlook.com)
> Report a bug or suggest an improvement in [GitHub issues](https://github.com/onlook-dev/onlook/issues).
> **Earlier versions.** Onlook v1, the browser-based editor for Next.js and Tailwind projects, is on the [`v1` branch](https://github.com/onlook-dev/onlook/tree/v1). The first Onlook desktop app is at [onlook-dev/desktop](https://github.com/onlook-dev/desktop).
