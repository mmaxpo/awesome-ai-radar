# Contributing

Thanks for helping make the radar better!

## Suggest a repo

The easiest way: **[open a "Suggest a repo" issue](../../issues/new?template=suggest-repo.yml)**.
Paste the link, pick a category, and write one or two sentences on why it's useful.
When it's approved, a bot adds it to the list and closes the issue — you don't need to touch any files.

## What makes a good pick

- **Open source** and on GitHub
- **Useful to people building with AI** — a tool, framework, model, or great learning resource
- **Alive** — maintained, or a finished classic worth studying (like nanoGPT)
- Not a duplicate, a thin wrapper, or a project that only promotes a paid product

## Other ways to help

- Spot a wrong category, a dead repo, or a note that could be clearer? Open an issue.
- Improvements to the script (`scripts/update.py`) are welcome as pull requests.
- Want your own radar? Click **Use this template** at the top of the repo.

## For the maintainer

On a suggestion issue, comment:

- `/approve` — add it with the category the suggester picked
- `/approve rag` — add it under a different category
- Otherwise, just close the issue with a short note.
